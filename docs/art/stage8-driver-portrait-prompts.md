# Stage 8 Driver Portrait Prompt Provenance

Both portrait sheets were created with Codex's built-in image-generation tool.
The generated source PNGs were preserved byte-for-byte beside this document;
the 1024×1024 runtime copies under `Assets/Resources/UI` were derived from
those sources without redesigning the artwork.

## Founders sheet — final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: Unity game UI driver portrait sprite
sheet. Create one square 2×2 sheet containing exactly four original RC-club
driver busts for a playful South American neighborhood racing club. Use a
genuinely transparent background, equal quadrants, generous transparent
gutters, consistent head/shoulder scale, and no overlap.

- Top-left: young adult woman with dense dark curls, yellow bandana, soldering
  goggles pushed up, and an upbeat competitive grin.
- Top-right: older man with a salt-and-pepper moustache, teal mechanic cap,
  worn work shirt, and kind mischievous expression.
- Bottom-left: young adult nonbinary member with a short undercut, red workshop
  jacket, hearing protection around the neck, and focused expression.
- Bottom-right: middle-aged woman with a long dark braid, blue workshop apron,
  and determined amused expression.

Use original energetic hand-inked 1990s animation cues: chunky expressive
outlines, playful facial construction, imperfect brush texture, limited cel
shading, and strong readability at 96 pixels. Use workshop yellow, tape red,
teal-blue, charcoal, and warm off-white. Do not copy protected characters or
shows. No text, logos, flags, national emblems, vehicles, scenery, borders,
watermarks, drop shadows, extra figures, or content crossing quadrant gutters.

## Neighbors sheet — base generation prompt

Use the founders sheet only as a style, palette, scale, framing, and line-
quality reference. Create a second square 2×2 transparent sheet with exactly
three new portraits and an entirely empty bottom-right quadrant:

- Top-left: age-appropriate teenage electronics tinkerer in a purple hoodie
  with safety goggles and an eager inventive expression.
- Top-right: adult mother and club organizer in a green workshop jumpsuit,
  carrying her fully clothed baby securely in a front carrier; both wear
  hearing protection.
- Bottom-left: young adult mechanic in an orange shirt with a natural dark mole
  precisely centered on her forehead and a calm daring expression.

Match the founders sheet's original hand-inked treatment and exact quadrant
geometry. No text, logos, flags, national emblems, vehicles, scenery, borders,
watermarks, exposed tools in hands, extra figures, or content in the fourth
quadrant.

## Neighbors sheet — final revision prompt

Edit only the three portraits while preserving the sheet layout, transparent
gutters, framing, scale, palette, mother identity, and rendering style:

- Redesign the top-left tinkerer as an unmistakably male teenager aged roughly
  15–16, with a medium-brown complexion, distinct facial structure, short
  textured dark hair, purple hoodie, safety goggles, and inventive grin.
- Keep the top-right mother unchanged; change only the baby to light brown or
  dark-blond hair and light hazel-green eyes, remaining safely supported,
  clothed, content, and protected by hearing gear.
- Keep the bottom-left pose, orange shirt, expression, and framing; give her a
  distinctly pale complexion, golden-blonde wavy hair, lighter eyebrows, and a
  larger natural dark beauty mark precisely centered on her forehead.
- Keep the bottom-right quadrant completely empty and transparent.

The teenager must remain age-appropriate and non-sexualized. The beauty mark
must read as a mole, not jewelry or paint. Add no text, logos, flags, national
emblems, scenery, borders, watermarks, extra figures, or gutter-crossing art.
